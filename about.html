My story

From building software to improving how organizations operate.

I studied Computer Science at Osun State University, graduating with Second Class Upper honours in 2023. The technical foundation was valuable, but the most important lesson came from delivery work: software only creates value when it fits the way an organization actually works.

Since then I have delivered digital products and platforms across several organizations, coordinating cross-functional teams, translating business goals into requirements, improving delivery processes and keeping technical infrastructure reliable. That experience taught me to start every project with the business, not the technology.

In October 2026 I joined Pacific Maritime Limited as IT Officer, where my work is closer to that of a digital operations analyst: understanding how marine operations run and where systems can help. I delivered an Equipment Compliance System now in operational use, and I am developing the Pacific Maritime Operating System to connect rental operations with finance visibility.

I am currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Port Harcourt. My direction is clear: to contribute to the transformation of energy and industrial organizations through business understanding, technology innovation and practical implementation.

How I work

A business-first sequence.

Every engagement follows the same discipline: understanding before software.

Read the full methodology
  1. Understand the organizationGoals, structure and day-to-day realities.
  2. Learn the processesWhere time, clarity or consistency is lost.
  3. Identify opportunitiesWhere technology creates practical improvement.
  4. Design practical solutionsFitted to how the organization actually works.
  5. Measure resultsAgainst the business goals the work serves.
