Marine Operations · Operational Systems · Process Integration

Pacific Maritime Operating System (PMOS)

One connected workflow for equipment rental, so operations and finance see the same request, the same approvals and the same status at every step.

Sector
Marine & offshore operations
Workflow
8 connected stages
Status
In development
Focus
Coordination & authorization visibility

01Overview

An operational system being developed to connect internal rental operations with finance visibility, turning a multi-team process into a single, traceable workflow.

02Business context

Renting out marine equipment is not one task but a chain of them. A request arrives, the equipment is inspected, a contract is approved, finance raises an invoice and confirms payment, and only then is the equipment released, deployed and eventually returned.

Each step belongs to a different team, and each depends on the one before it.

03Operational challenge

When a process spans operations, management and finance, the hard part is coordination. Before equipment moves, teams need to know that the right approvals and payment confirmations are in place, and that knowledge must be visible, not assumed or chased by phone.

04Opportunity identified

Model the full rental lifecycle as one workflow with defined stages, owners and authorizations, so information flows between operations and finance instead of around them.

The rental lifecycle PMOS connects

Eight stages, from first request to equipment back in the yard.

  1. Rental requestOperations receives and records the request
  2. InspectionEquipment condition is checked before commitment
  3. Contract approvalAuthorization is granted and visible
  4. Finance invoicingFinance raises the invoice from the same record
  5. Payment confirmationPayment status is confirmed in the workflow
  6. Equipment releaseRelease follows confirmed authorization
  7. Deployment trackingWhere the equipment is, while it is out
  8. Equipment returnThe cycle closes when equipment comes back

05Design priorities

  • Operational coordination across teams
  • Authorization visibility before equipment moves
  • Workflow consistency for every rental
  • Information flow between operations and finance

06My role

Mapping how the interconnected rental, approval and finance processes actually work, and designing and developing an operational system around them.

  • Process understanding across operations and finance
  • Workflow and authorization design
  • System design
  • Development (in progress)

07Intended value

A shared, trustworthy view of every rental: who requested it, what was approved, whether it was paid for, and where the equipment is now.

PMOS is in active development. This page describes its design intent and workflow; results will be documented once the system is in operational use.
