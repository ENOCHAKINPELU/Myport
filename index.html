Introduction

Technology creates value only when it fits how the operation actually runs.

My work starts on the operational side: how equipment moves, how approvals flow, where information gets stuck between field, office and finance. From there I identify where a digital system would genuinely improve visibility and coordination, and where it would only add complexity.

I have a Computer Science background and practical experience designing, building and deploying business systems. Today I apply that capability to energy, marine and industrial operations, turning operational understanding into solutions people use every day.

The approach

From operational reality to business value.

Every project follows the same sequence. Business understanding comes first; technology comes in only where it earns its place.

  1. 01

    Business understanding

    How the organization creates value, who does the work and which decisions matter most.

  2. 02

    Operational challenges

    Where time, visibility or control is lost: manual records, unclear hand-offs, delayed information.

  3. 03

    Technology opportunities

    Where a digital system would genuinely help, and where it would only add another tool.

  4. 04

    Solution design

    Workflows, roles, data and approvals designed around the real process, not a template.

  5. 05

    Implementation

    Building, deploying and validating with the people who will use the system every day.

  6. 06

    Business value

    Clearer visibility, consistent workflows and better-informed operational decisions.
