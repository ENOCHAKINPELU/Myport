Hospitality operationsHotelOps.ngClearer operations. More accountable teams.

HotelOps.ng is a hospitality operations platform that helps hotels achieve operational excellence through better visibility, accountability and execution. Many hotels still rely on WhatsApp groups, verbal instructions and manual follow-ups to coordinate daily operations, leading to poor staff accountability, delayed issue resolution and inconsistent guest experiences. HotelOps.ng gives owners and managers real-time control over what is happening across the property without constant follow-ups.

What I delivered

  • A real-time task management system with clear ownership and completion tracking across departments.
  • An issue resolution workflow to surface and escalate problems before they impact guests.
  • An operations dashboard giving managers instant visibility across the property.
  • Multi-property support to standardize execution across locations.

My contribution

Led product strategy and roadmap definition. Conducted interviews with hotel owners and operations managers to identify key friction points. Oversaw feature prioritization, sprint planning and go-to-market positioning for the Nigerian hospitality market.

Tools & technologies

React, Node.js, PostgreSQL, Firebase, Figma
